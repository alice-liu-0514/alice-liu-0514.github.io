## Overview

This project explores how AI might support case conceptualization for practicing psychologists. The design is grounded in real clinical supervision practices and treats AI as a reflective partner, not an authority.

## Design direction

The system is intended to help psychologists consider a case through multiple layers and across orientations. Its role is to expand the set of possible interpretations and questions a practitioner can consider, while leaving evaluation, decisions, and professional responsibility with the psychologist.
