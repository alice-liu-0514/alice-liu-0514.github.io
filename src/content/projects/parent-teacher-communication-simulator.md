## The need

Group interviews with teachers surfaced a gap between teacher training and the realities of difficult parent-teacher conversations.

## What we built

The team built a simulator that lets educators practise these conversations in a risk-free setting, with dynamically changing emotional responses.

## Recognition

The project received the Golden Prize, Cheng Yu-Wei Education Technology Award.
